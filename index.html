How many treats can I give my dog per day?
Treats should make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily calorie intake. For a medium-sized dog (30-50 lbs), this typically means 8-12 treats per day. Always adjust based on your dog's size, activity level, and regular food intake. Consult your vet for specific recommendations.
